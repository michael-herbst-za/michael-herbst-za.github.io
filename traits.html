01 What I Can Be Trusted To Do

As the only technical person

Comfortable as the only technical person in a small organisation, equally at home resolving a user's ticket and administering the server behind it.

As the single point of accountability

Comfortable as the single point of accountability for a full IT estate, from a single site to a multi-office, multi-location organisation, and equally comfortable fixing it personally.

As the owner of the whole stack

Comfortable owning the whole stack as the sole technical resource, and equally comfortable documenting it so someone else can.
